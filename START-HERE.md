# Start here

For a Singapore business owner, or whoever helps them with the computer.

No jargon below. If a line does not make sense, skip it; nothing here breaks anything.

---

## What this is

Singapore businesses using AI to help with the books keep hitting the same problems. The AI
assumes American tax rules. It invents numbers that look confident and are wrong. It promises a
customer a refund the owner never agreed to.

This fixes those three things, for Singapore.

**It does not file anything.** Your GST return still goes through IRAS-approved software or the
myTax Portal. This helps you work out the numbers and draft the words. A person still presses
submit.

## Two ways to use it. Pick one.

### Way 1: copy and paste. No install, no sign-up, five minutes.

Open **[RECIPES.md](RECIPES.md)**. Copy the "Master" block, paste it into whatever AI you already
use (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, anything), then paste the task you want underneath.

That is the whole thing. It works on a phone. Your helper can set it up once and you reuse it.

**Start here if you are not sure.** It costs you nothing and you will know within ten minutes
whether this is useful for your business.

### Way 2: install it into Claude. About five minutes, no terminal.

Once installed it works on its own, so you do not have to remember to paste anything.

1. Open Claude and sign in.
2. In the left sidebar, click **Customize**.
3. Click the **Plugins** tab.
4. Click **Browse plugins**.
5. Add this as a marketplace: `lobang-scout/sg-sme-plugin`
6. Find **sg-sme** in the list and click **Install**.

That is it. Ask a normal question and the right help switches on by itself. You can also type `/`
or press `+` to pick one on purpose.

> **If you get stuck at any step, stop and use Way 1 instead.** It does the same thinking. The
> install only saves you the pasting.

## What to try first

Pick whichever sounds like your Monday.

**"I have no system, only a notebook."**
Take a photo of the page and ask: *"Here is my takings book for last week. What is my break-even
and can I cover rent?"* It reads handwriting, tells you what it cannot read, and never guesses a
number.

**"Can I make payroll next month?"**
Give it whatever you have. A bank screenshot, a supplier WhatsApp thread, a spreadsheet in
whatever shape you keep it. It works out what is coming in, what is already owed, and how long
the money lasts.

**"A customer is angry and I do not know what to write."**
Paste the message. You get a reply ready to send that **promises nothing**, plus a short list of
the decisions only you can make, like whether to offer a refund. You decide, then the line goes
in.

**"Do I need to do this InvoiceNow thing?"**
Ask. Every GST-registered business gets caught by 2031, most owners do not know their date, and
getting set up takes three to twelve months. So the date to act on is a year before your
deadline. It will tell you how to find yours rather than guess it.

## What it will not do

Being clear about this up front, because the opposite wastes your afternoon.

- **It will not file your GST.** That stays with IRAS-approved software or myTax Portal.
- **It will not promise anything to a customer** without you approving it first. That is
  deliberate.
- **It will not make up a number** it cannot see. If your photo only covers half a month, it says
  so instead of quietly filling the rest.
- **It is not your accountant.** For anything that decides a tax position, confirm with a person
  who is responsible for the answer.

## About the numbers in it

Singapore rules change. CPF rates change again on 1 January 2027. GST went from 8% to 9% in 2024.
InvoiceNow is still rolling out to 2031.

So every figure here carries the date it was checked, and the tool is told to **warn you if that
date is getting old** before it gives you a number. If it says a figure may be out of date,
believe it and check the official page.

Sources are always IRAS, CPF Board, PDPC or IMDA. Never a blog, never us.
